1. Download the official 4x4Tools-Setup.exe from this project's GitHub release.
2. Save your projects and close After Effects, Premiere and Media Encoder.
3. Run Setup, review the terms and choose video effects, audio effects, or both.
   Setup downloads the runtime and your GPU's encrypted model payloads.
   For offline setup, put release .4x4data assets in a Data folder beside Setup.
4. In Adobe video effects, look under 4x4Tools.
5. In Premiere, open Preferences > Audio > Audio Plug-In Manager and scan
   VST3 plug-ins. Look for the 4x4 audio effects from vendor 4x4Tools.

VIDEO
Eye Contact, Background Remove, Background Blur, Studio Light,
Video Denoise, Auto Frame and Detail Restore.
Background Remove creates transparency: place your replacement background
on a layer/track underneath. Highest Quality is the default.

AUDIO
Noise Clean, Room Echo Clean, Voice Clean, Studio Voice Preview,
Studio Voice HQ and Voice Restore. All are native AE effects and VST3 effects.
Use Studio Voice HQ for rendered work; its model analyzes six-second blocks.
Premiere receives the processing latency for compensation. For live editing,
start with Voice Clean or Studio Voice Preview.

An existing standalone 4x4 Eye Contact installation is retained. Fresh suite
installations include Eye Contact. No sign-in or NGC key is required to run.

Requires Windows x64, supported NVIDIA RTX/T4 hardware and a current driver
with CUDA 12.8 support. Models are included for Turing, Ampere, Ada and
Blackwell. RTX 5070 is the hardware validation machine.

Read QUALITY-REPORT.md and USER-GUIDE.md for status and current limitations.
Speaker Focus is excluded because its model requires additional NGC access.

Uninstall from Windows Installed Apps: 4x4Tools Broadcast Suite.

# Third-party components

4x4 Eye Contact integrates NVIDIA Augmented Reality SDK 1.2.0.0 and its Gaze
Redirection, Face Landmark Detection and Face Box Detection features.

NVIDIA's runtime, model and bundled-library license documents are retained
under `runtime/ARSDK`. Their terms apply to those components. SDK headers,
development libraries, download archives and credentials are excluded.

NVIDIA trademarks belong to NVIDIA Corporation. Adobe, Premiere and After
Effects trademarks belong to Adobe. 4x4Tools publishes this plugin.

Developer verification uses NVIDIA's public AR sample recording. The sample
and comparison images are not included in the installer; its sample-data
license remains in `third_party/AR-SDK-Samples`.
